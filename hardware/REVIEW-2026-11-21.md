schematic readability). Findings below are consolidated and deduplicated; tags: DS datasheet, CALC computed, EST estimate.
Status column (updated 2026-11-21 after the fixes): DONE = applied (commit), OPEN = needs your decision, FW = firmware rule in
`FIRMWARE-RULES.md`.

## C: cleanup (all mechanical) -- DONE d3652dd except where noted
Stale text: 10 W panel values in power-board board.yaml and the PV note ("up to 1.7 A"), REQUIREMENTS-system.md 3.1 (header is 18 pins with D+/D-), 5.2 (pull-ups 2.2 k), SYS window 1.8-5.5 V (not 1.5), title-block date 2026-10-31 hard-coded in schkit.py, BOM Status "VERIFIED 2026-10-31" on parts added later, "SHT45 cable" comment covers J3 and J4. C90/C91 use C14663 (0603) where C307331 (0402) is on the BOM. C47 fitted though U8 not fitted. J11/J12 missing from the hand-solder list. J7 pin 1 is +3V3: label "3.3 V only". L3 pin numbering conflicts inside the EMTEK datasheet: ohmmeter check at incoming inspection. QON test pad. REGN C5 0603 4.7 uF about 2.5 uF effective at 5 V (use 0805). J8 pinout and pitch unverified (no ZE25-O3 datasheet in the repo); J2 row orientation must be checked against the real cable; U9 pad 9 is a no-copper laser-mark pad; U9 and the BMP585 add-on both use 0x46 (never fit both). Header comment/ARCHITECTURE wording errors (GND rows, "I2C rows").

## Readability findings (generator parameters) -- DONE 80e46e6, cf613d4, 5fb1801 except where noted
- Root pages show no power flow (SYS, VBUS, BAT_PACK, USB_VBUS are symbols): add drawn arrows or notes between root boxes (ROOT_TEXTS, schkit root writer).
- PWR_FLAG stubs look like stray parts (put on a wire of their net or rotate), refs overlap their own symbol (J5, D1, J6, JP1/JP2, U4/U5/U10/U11: TAG_POS), notes run past the frame (wrap notes in boardgen draw), J10 labels have no direction shape and pass through the connector body, connector polarity not written (PV IN 1+ 2-, CELL 1+ 2-), J12 pin order not on the sheet (value RAIN/WIND/VANE), spurious junction dot on GAS_RXIN, PM_RXIN/PM_SETIN labels ambiguous, outdoor-cable ESD not visibly between J3 and the bus, programming passives of U3 reach pins only by label, no reason given for open ESP32 pins, ref/value of USBLC6 overlap wires, EN wire of U7 drawn through a GND symbol text.
- Suggested partition: power board 3 sheets (Inputs and J10, Charger, Battery), main board 5 sheets (USB-C and J10, MCU, Rails, I2C sensor bus, UART/analog/pulse sensors). Sheet names are part of net paths: update critical.yaml and split_check.py together.
- review_checks.py should learn: ref/value box overlapping its own symbol body, note box beyond frame, rail symbol pointing down on a non-GND rail.
